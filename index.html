I am a junior undergraduate studying Computer Science at the University of Michigan and pursuing a dual degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, with an expected graduation in 2027.

My research focuses on embodied AI, robot learning, and vision-language models. I am interested in building scalable methods that give robots high-level semantic understanding while retaining the precision needed for adaptive manipulation in unseen environments.

I am actively searching for PhD opportunities in Robotics starting Fall 2027.

selected publications

  1. ICRA
    Topology-Informed Visual Prompting for Vision-Language-Action Policies
    Haoyang Wu, Abhinav Kumar, and Dmitry Berenson
    In submission to IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation, 2027
    Abs arXiv PDF Website

    We use simulated planning and topological reasoning to generate additional training trajectories, then visually prompt vision-language-action policies with predicted robot waypoints during deployment. The method improves performance across simulated and real-world tasks, increasing hardware success by 40 percent over the strongest baseline.

  2. ECCV
    Vero: An Open RL Recipe for General Visual Reasoning
    Gabriel Sarch, Linrong Cai, Qunzhong Wang, Haoyang Wu, Danqi Chen, and Zhuang Liu
    In European Conference on Computer Vision, 2026
    Abs arXiv PDF Website

    Vero is a family of fully open vision-language models for general visual reasoning. By scaling reinforcement-learning data with the 600K-sample Vero-600K dataset and task-routed rewards, Vero achieves state-of-the-art performance across 30 challenging benchmarks.

  3. TMI
    Holistic Surgical Phase Recognition with Hierarchical Input-Dependent State Space Models
    Haoyang Wu, Tsun-Hsuan Wang, Mathias Lechner, Ramin Hasani, Jennifer A. Eckhoff, Paul Pak, Ozanan R. Meireles, Guy Rosman, Yutong Ban, and Daniela Rus
    In submission to IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging, 2025
    Abs arXiv PDF Website

    We propose a hierarchical input-dependent state space model for decision-making over full-length surgical videos. The framework combines a temporally consistent visual encoder with local aggregation and global relation modules for fine-grained dynamics and long-range dependencies.
